Mechanical Design Prototype Science Fair

Frictionless Wind Turbine Project

Devised and modeled a magnetic-levitation wind turbine using active magnetic bearings and magnetic gears to eliminate mechanical friction losses. Assembled a working prototype from the ground up and presented the research at the Dallas Regional Science Fair, advancing as a Top 48 finalist out of roughly 1,100 competitors.

Research Applied Math

Maglev Train Mathematical Modeling

Modeled electrodynamic suspension (EDS) maglev levitation using differential equations, Fourier series, and the Lorentz and London equations. Applied Euler and RK4 numerical methods in Python to derive real-world acceleration and load-safety parameters.

AI / ML Healthcare

Skin Cancer Diagnostic AI Model

Engineered an AI diagnostic model reaching 98.8% accuracy in skin cancer detection and integrated it into a mobile app prototype. Qualified for the Texas State Science & Engineering Fair.

About me

A bit about
who I am

I'm Senai Sorsa, a first-semester Aerospace Engineering student at Texas A&M University. Over a summer as a NASA SEES intern, I researched sustainable urban development using satellite and geospatial data, co-authored work accepted to the American Geophysical Union Fall Meeting, and presented findings to more than 100 NASA and UT Austin faculty.

Outside the classroom, I founded and lead a 30+ member robotics and programming club, mentor debate delegates through YMCA Youth and Government, and build hands-on engineering projects — from a magnetic-levitation wind turbine to AI diagnostic models. I graduated from Imagine International Academy of North Texas with a STEM Endorsement Diploma and a 4.629 weighted GPA.
