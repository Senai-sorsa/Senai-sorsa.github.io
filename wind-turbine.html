Featured Build

Two-Stage Spur Gearbox

A small gearbox built to actually put assembly mates through their paces instead of just modeling static parts sitting next to each other. Each shaft seats in its bearing bore with a concentric and coincident mate, and the two spur gears are tied together with a gear mate so the rotation ratio holds once the assembly is driven in a motion study. The housing started as a simple extruded shell, then picked up counterbored corner holes once the fastening was worked out. This was the project where an assembly actually ran instead of just sitting there looking like it might.

Lofted Cutlery Spoon

A single-part exercise in surface transitions. The bowl and handle are two very different cross sections, so this uses a loft between profile sketches with guide curves controlling the taper, plus fillets smoothing the neck where the two shapes meet.

Sheet Metal Mounting Bracket

An L shaped bracket built with the sheet metal tools instead of modeling it as a solid and hoping the bends would work out. Base and edge flanges set the material thickness and bend radius, and the flat pattern confirmed the folds lined up before anything gets cut.

Curved Rail Soap Stand

A half-moon frame with rails swept along a 3D sketch rather than a flat profile, since the rim curves in two directions at once. Each rail sweeps along its own guide curve, then patterns across the frame so the spacing stays even.
